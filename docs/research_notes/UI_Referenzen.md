## 1. The most important patterns for Schlusswerk

1. **Deduction per line with a reason from a fixed list and a mandatory note** (BuildPass, Oracle Textura). Per item: submitted, approved, difference, reason (e.g. "work not complete", "defect", "change order not commissioned", "overvalued") and a mandatory note. Below it the cascade down to the payout amount. → **Pre-check from the general contractor's (GU) perspective**, later **final meeting sheet (Schlussbesprechungs-Blatt)**. G
2. **Review status as a colour per value: yellow = machine, green = checked by a human, red = blocked** (Rossum). Red prevents completion. → **Ledger** and **approval**: export only once no red cell is open. G
3. **Every cell carries answer, calculation, quotes and a review status; after approval it is locked** (Harvey Review Tables). In addition comments and coloured flags per cell, recalculating a single cell, full-screen preview of the original. → **Ledger**, **finding/evidence panel**, **approval**. G
4. **Only input columns are white, everything else is calculated** (Siteline). → **Ledger**: humans only change quantity or approval, totals and differences are grey and not editable. G
5. **Plan index comparison as an overlay plus plain text per change, also for changes without a cloud** (Trunk Tools TrunkReview; PlanRadar shows changes in colour side by side). → **Evidence panel** for additional work from a plan change; candidates for **"angemeldet und offen"** (notified and open). G
6. **Status snapshot per change order at every billing** (Payapps "Variation History") and **"approved below offer" as a separate marker** (Siteline). → Lists **"angemeldet und offen"** and **"verloren"** (lost): you see when a change order was submitted, cut or never answered. G / S
7. **Triage by keyboard: 1 accept, 2 discard (with reason), 3 duplicate, H snooze** (Linear). → **Approval** of the findings line by line, fast and without a mouse. G
8. **Completeness lock before submission** (GCPay: mandatory documents must be present before submission is possible). → **Cockpit/export**: checklist "fehlt noch" (still missing) (e.g. test reports, as-built plans) before the final invoice (Schlussrechnung). S (GCPay), A (Austrian documents)

## 2. New on reebuild and Kuro

**Honestly up front:** For neither of them did I find **demo videos, webinars or help pages** publicly. reebuild only links LinkedIn, no YouTube, no blog, no help centre; Kuro has references but no videos (G, [reebuild.com](https://www.reebuild.com), [kuro.technology](https://www.kuro.technology)). The job ads of both companies do not describe the interface (G, [JOIN reebuild](https://join.com/companies/reebuild), [Kuro Join us](https://www.kuro.technology/join-us)). **What follows from this (A):** RN1 is the most important finding. The GU reviews of earlier partial invoices (correction sheet, review note) are the best indication of what the GU will cut in the final invoice. KN5 confirms our approach "finding first, no chat": Kuro also starts with ready answers to a fixed list of questions.

## 4. Competition note: billing tools for subcontractors

| Statement | Mark | Source |
|---|---|---|
| **Siteline** is built on US law and US forms: AIA forms, lien waivers, lien deadlines per state, 23,000+ GU forms from 17,000+ GUs | G | [siteline.com](https://siteline.com/) |
| No indication of Europe, German or expansion to Europe | S (search without results) | – |
| **Payapps** operates in Australia, New Zealand, UK and Ireland (acquisition of WebContractor 2023); rules according to Australian and New Zealand payment law | G / S | [payapps.com](https://www.payapps.com/features/), [Press release 2023](https://bebeez.eu/2023/10/09/payapps-expands-uk-and-ireland-footprint-with-strategic-acquisition-of-webcontractor/) |
| **GCPay, Textura, BuildPass** are portals operated by the **GU**; the subcontractor submits there. No review from the subcontractor's perspective | G / S | No. 1, 2, 6 above |
| All these tools manage **ongoing partial payments** (monthly, percent complete). None builds an **evidence chain from quantity survey, daywork, plans, emails** and none predicts what the GU will cut | A (from the pages read) | – |
| In the DACH region I found **no AI tool on the subcontractor side** for the final invoice. Close: plancraft (quantity survey by voice, trades), Phase0 (tendering, award, billing according to HOAI), Offpaper (forms from the construction site) | S | [Search result plancraft](https://www.ki-syndikat.de/tools/plancraft/), [Phase0](https://www.starting-up.de/news/news-investments/phase0-erhaelt-zusaetzliches-seedkapital.html) |
| **Conclusion:** Siteline and Payapps are not competitors in Austria today (language, law, ÖNORM B 2110 missing). **reebuild** is more dangerous: it also sells material and subcontractor invoice review to "Bauunternehmen" (construction companies), so it may already be at our target customers (electrical/HVAC/plumbing with 30–150 people) and has the data of the GU side | A | reebuild.com/bauunternehmen |
