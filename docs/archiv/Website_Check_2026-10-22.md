## Result in five sentences

1. The hero passes the 10-second test for the "what": "Schlussrechnungen, die nichts liegen lassen" (final invoices that leave nothing behind) plus subline and "Demo buchen" (book a demo) are clear; the owner only loses the thread in the solution section ("digitaler Zwilling" (digital twin), "AVA", "Ebene 1 / Ebene 2" (level 1 / level 2)) and nowhere finds what he himself has to do or hand over.
2. The worst error is in the content: for the same "Projekt Wien 21" the page shows **+5.546 €** once (timeline) and **+27.410 €** once (result), and in the product Wien 21 is an **HKLS** (HVAC/plumbing) lot, while the website shows sockets and luminaires (electrical) there.
3. The page promises more than the product may deliver: "kein verdienter Euro vergessen" (no earned euro forgotten) and "Alle 6 Änderungen belegt und verrechnet" (all 6 changes backed by evidence and billed), including a daywork (Regie) item **without a signature**, and the trust section (human confirms, evidence by click, we never talk to your general contractor (GU), data silo) has been removed entirely since v2.
4. The product makes sense, but the strongest objection hits exactly the flagship: a voice note (Zuruf) that was never written down is not in any folder either, so a retroactive run can hardly find it; whether the money lies more in emails, plans and daywork slips must be shown by R2/R3, and the page should follow that.
5. Rules are largely followed (no "Claim", no prices, no "Check anfragen" (request a Check), no dashes); open are visible placeholders on a publicly accessible page, "·" as a separator, the CTA "Demo buchen" contrary to `CLAUDE.md`, and a dummy calendar that offers appointments that do not exist.

## 3. Does the product make sense? The three strongest objections

**In short: yes, as a tool for the invoicing clerk before the final invoice. Whether anyone pays for it depends on objection A, whether it delivers technically on objection B.** Nothing from dossier 12 is proposed again here.

### Objection A: "Mein Projektleiter oder Abrechner macht das schon. Und was am Ende fehlt, tauschen wir sowieso in der Schlussbesprechung." (My project manager or invoicing clerk already does that. And whatever is missing at the end, we trade off in the final meeting anyway.)
- **Strength: high.** The pain is not proven for Austria ("Keine österreichische Quelle zu Schlussrechnungskürzungen gefunden" (no Austrian source on final invoice deductions found), `Zielgruppe_Challenge.md` section 4). Dossier 2 itself names the deliberate trade-off as a cause whose size only R3 measures. The page ignores this and shows "Alle 6 Änderungen belegt und verrechnet".
- **What refutes it:** a **blind comparison** in the Check (R2/R3): the invoicing clerk writes his list for a project as always, then ours comes. Count: euros that only we found, per project, against his own state as the blind value. In addition the five R1 questions from `Zielgruppe_Challenge.md` section 5 (passed with at least 5 of 8 with a euro amount). On the page: instead of "verrechnet" (billed), honestly "belegt, die Entscheidung liegt bei Ihnen" (backed by evidence, the decision is yours); that also takes the trade-off seriously.

### Objection B: "Was nie aufgeschrieben wurde, findet auch eure KI nicht." (What was never written down, your AI will not find either.)
- **Strength: high, and logical, not just a feeling.** The lead hypothesis says the money lies in the informal: phone, voice note, WhatsApp (dossier 2, marked as an assumption). A retroactive run over a project folder only sees what happened to land in an email or a photo. The voice note route via WhatsApp only comes from stage 2, i.e. after the gates (dossier 0 point 3, dossier 15). But the page advertises exactly such a case in its first image: "Per Zuruf vom Bauherrn beauftragt, nie verrechnet" (commissioned by the client by word of mouth, never billed) (hero, card 01). A sceptical owner asks: "Woher wisst ihr davon, wenn es nirgends steht?" (How do you know about it if it is not written anywhere?) (assumption).
- **What refutes it:** the source coding from R3 (dossier 15.5): what share of the euros found comes from emails, plans, quantity survey (Aufmaß) and daywork slips that are already in the folder, and what share from voice notes? If the majority lies in existing documents, the product is sellable today and the page should lead with email and plan change cases. Until then: replace hero card 01 with a case that can be backed by evidence from a folder (e.g. move the outlets ordered by email from card 02 to the front). In addition have the insider check professionally whether "Bauherr" (client/owner) instead of "Bauleitung des Auftraggebers" (the client's site management) seems coherent for subcontractors; I do not assess this legally (`CLAUDE.md` rule 5).

### Objection C: "Ich schicke dem GU keine Rechnung, die eine KI zusammengesucht hat. Eine falsche Position kostet mich mehr Vertrauen, als sie bringt." (I will not send the GU an invoice that an AI has put together. One wrong item costs me more trust than it brings.) (plus: "Wer sieht meine Daten?" (who sees my data?))
- **Strength: medium.** The product is built for this: AI delivers facts with a source, the human confirms, the invoice is deterministic code, every number leads to the original (`CLAUDE.md` rule 7; demo app with two-step approval, `_STATUS.md` product 22.10.). The problem is that the **page** shows none of this. The principles section was removed in v2 (`STRUKTUR.md` update v2), and the solution promises "prüft damit jede Schlussrechnung" (thereby checks every final invoice) without mentioning the human.
- **What refutes it:** on the page: four sentences of trust (you confirm every item; every number with a click to the original; we never talk to your client; your data stays in your area, only released projects). In the test: measure the error rate, i.e. the share of items we propose that the invoicing clerk discards in the Check. Gate: R5 (does anyone pay) is the actual proof.

**Additionally for investors (read):** the first market is small, roughly 150 to 350 businesses per trade in Vienna and Lower Austria (`Zielgruppe_Challenge.md` section 1, itself marked as an assumption). The page does not have to solve this, but the pitch material should show that HVAC/plumbing and other federal states follow without restructuring.

## 5. Quality compared to Linear, Attio, reebuild: the three spots that look most like a template or AI

Comparison from my knowledge of the pages, not reopened today, therefore an **assessment (assumption)**. What the three role models have in common: they show their real product large and early, and every number or claim sits in a real screen.

1. **Hero with stock photos and floating cards.** Three Unsplash images (`public/hero/QUELLEN.md`: open socket, plans, construction workers with tablet) with white cards "+ 780 €" on top. The pattern "photo plus floating UI card" is the most common template hero; the socket looks like an old building renovation, not like residential construction for a GU, and construction workers with a tablet are the cliché image par excellence. **Proposal:** a real excerpt from the demo app (`product/app`, finding page with the original evidence side by side), synthetic data visibly marked. A click on the number opens the evidence: show "Jede Zahl führt zum Original" (every number leads to the original) instead of saying it. Effort medium.
2. **Result card with five blue ticks and a counting-up amount.** "Vergessene Regie gefunden ✓ Mehrmengen erkannt ✓ Planänderungen eingerechnet ✓ Vorab geprüft wie vom Auftraggeber ✓ Zu jeder Position ein Beleg ✓" (forgotten daywork found ✓ additional quantities recognised ✓ plan changes included ✓ checked in advance as by the client ✓ evidence for every item ✓) plus counter "+ €0 → 27.410". Tick lists and counters are AI landing page standard and say nothing verifiable. The same pattern repeats at "Demo buchen" with three ticks. **Proposal:** instead of ticks, three real lines of a final invoice with item, quantity, amount and one evidence icon each (what Wien 21 really contains according to `Demo_Faelle.md`), amount static. Effort small.
3. **Typography kit: mono section numbers "02 · PROBLEM" and two-colour headline.** Small mono capitals with number and middle dot above every section, headline with the second line in accent colour ("Schlussrechnungen, / die nichts liegen lassen."), "Wer EndPoint. baut." (who builds EndPoint.) with four identical "Co-Founder" cards. You see this on very many generated pages in 2025/26. The plan grid is distinctive, the rest is not. **Proposal:** actually use the dimension chains from the CI (`STRUKTUR.md` section 2, "Leitmotiv Maßketten" (dimension chain motif)) as section markers instead of "02 ·"; headline in one colour; in the team one line each on who does what, and as soon as R1 is in place, the construction insider visibly included (the insider card was removed in v2, but for a trade business owner it is the strongest trust signal next to four students, assumption). Effort small to medium.
